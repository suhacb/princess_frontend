Two ways to rebuild the shell

Every API call needs a project ID, so project context is the root of the app — you choose a project, then everything else is viewed through it. Below, the same product is mapped two ways. A · Cockpit keeps a stable, role-aware workspace with a scoped rail and a persistent AI dock. B · Context Canvas makes relationships the primary thing you navigate, with a command palette and AI woven into every item. Switch role and direction in the bar above; flip annotations off to read the screens clean.

Direction A

Cockpit — a stable workspace you live inside

A scoped left rail with the project picker pinned to the top, a top-bar switcher for jumping projects without losing your place, a role-aware Project Home, and a persistent right-side Princess dock that unifies all four AI surfaces. Familiar enough to onboard in minutes; the evolution is the role-lensing and the always-there AI.

Direction B

Context Canvas — navigate the relationships, not the menu

A slim rail and a ⌘K command palette as the primary mover. Items open as focused panels you traverse by relationship — risk → issue → change → document — so the data model becomes the navigation. AI is woven into every item (insight chips, an always-present ask bar, inline drafting) rather than living in a dock. Fewer chrome levels, more speed, very AI-native.

Side by side

Which way to commit? — the honest trade-offs

Both are project-first, role-aware and AI-native, and both treat documents as first-class links on every item. They differ in how you move and where the AI lives.

Direction A

Cockpit

Navigation

Stable scoped left rail + top-bar switcher. You browse by menu; structure is always visible.

Relationships

Shown as a small map on each item's detail page. You see them, then click in.

AI

A persistent right-side dock with four tabs — one obvious "place" for Princess.

Role

Re-prioritises the Home dashboard and trims rail groups per role.

Best for

Fast onboarding, governance comfort, users who want everything in sight. Lowest training cost.

Risk

Can feel familiar/heavier; relationship traversal is a few clicks, not the default motion.

Direction B

Context Canvas

Navigation

Slim rail + ⌘K palette. You move by searching and by walking relationship threads.

Relationships

Are the navigation — a thread spine you walk node to node. The model is the map.

AI

Diffused into every surface: insight chips, ask bar, inline editable drafts. Very native, less centralised.

Role

Reframes the whole canvas — a smaller, sharper graph of only what that role touches.

Best for

Power users, complex relationship webs, an AI-first product story. Feels genuinely 2026.

Risk

Steeper to learn; needs strong search + a clean graph or it gets disorienting.

A pragmatic path: ship A's stable shell first (low training cost, governance-friendly), but build it on a relationship + command-palette substrate so B's thread-walking and inline AI can layer in as power features. Project-first context, role lensing, doc-linking and the four AI surfaces are shared foundations either way.
